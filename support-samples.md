# Customer Support Replies

These practice scenarios use fictional orders and customer details.

## Late package | Email

**Customer**

> Hi, order #1048 was supposed to arrive yesterday. The tracking hasn’t moved since Monday. Is my package lost?

**What I checked**

The carrier picked up the package Monday. The estimated delivery date has passed, and there’s no newer scan. In this scenario, I’ve asked the shipping team to follow up with the carrier.

**Reply**

> Hi Mia,
>
> I checked order #1048. The last scan was when the carrier picked it up on Monday. It’s past the expected delivery date, so I’ve asked our shipping team to check with the carrier.
>
> I’ll email you by 3 p.m. Eastern tomorrow, even if we’re still waiting for an update. I’ll keep this open so you don’t have to start over with someone else.
>
> Thanks,  
> John

## Subscription renewal refund | Live chat

**Customer**

> I got charged for another box this morning. Can I cancel it and get my money back?

**What I checked**

The renewal order hasn’t shipped. In this scenario, the customer confirmed they wanted to cancel both the order and future renewals. The store allows a refund before shipment, and the refund has been issued.

**Reply**

> Hi Alex! I canceled today’s order and turned off future renewals. I’ve also issued the refund to the card you used. It can take a few business days to show up, depending on your bank. You’ll get an email confirmation for your records.
